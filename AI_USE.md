# AI use disclosure

OpenAI Codex provided substantial assistance with source research, acquisition
and analysis code, the executed notebook, the report, figures, documentation
and tests. The student supplied the assignment, course readings, prior-project
instructions and the requirement for an investment interpretation.

Codex read the locally supplied papers and prior assignment instructions,
retrieved public news/social/price records, constructed the equity basket and
text measures, estimated the statistical models, and prepared the written
analysis. Parallel agents assisted with data collection, source clocks,
language diagnostics, a 60-text qualitative review and independent software
checks. The qualitative review was AI-assisted development work, not independent
human gold-standard labeling; no sentiment-accuracy estimate is claimed.

VADER and Hu–Liu measures describe wording. Topic patterns were inspected and
refined for omitted safety/rogue-agent vocabulary; they were not trained on
returns. Current engagement metrics and language-detector outputs are explicitly
limited to descriptive checks or sensitivity analysis. All empirical numerical
results derive from acquired observations. Synthetic test fixtures are confined
to tests and never enter the study's data.

The observation window includes the September 30 closing prices and text
available through that day's 16:00 New York close. Later text is outside the
return window. Missing observations, inaccessible platforms, query caps,
short-sample uncertainty and the absence of a verified historical text vintage
are disclosed. No data or independent student verification was invented. No
trade was placed, and profitable out-of-sample performance is not claimed.
